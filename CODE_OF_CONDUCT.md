# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Giving and receiving feedback constructively, focusing on the work rather than the person
- Acknowledging mistakes and learning from them
- Being patient with contributors who are newer to SEO, Next.js, or open source
- Disagreeing with technical points and explaining why, with sources

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in issues, pull requests, or discussions
- Publishing private information (real name, email, location) of another contributor without their consent
- Dismissing contributions without explanation
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported by contacting the project maintainer directly through GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and investigated. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy of the reporter.

Consequences for violations may include a warning, a temporary ban from contributing, or a permanent ban, depending on severity and context.
